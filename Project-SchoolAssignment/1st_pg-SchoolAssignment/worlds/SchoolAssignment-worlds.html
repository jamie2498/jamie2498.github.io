Assignment: Express my manifesto(my own rule in mt life or my feature) into video.



Concept


I thought my room, which is my private space, is important to me as it can represent me. For example, when I have negative feeling, my room is also messy. When I clean my room, it makes me feel better. Also, this space, where was empty like white paper before I settled, is filled with my style now.

My private space and me are interacting each other, and we are finally being similar one. I wanted to express this realisation and process into collage video with simple performance.




Story Board

The video starts with empty white background by projector. I attach smaller pieces of paper on this big backgound and then each video is layered up on the papers. At the end, the pieces of videos gradually fade out.
